5. First 3 Launch Posts for the Page
Publish these 3 posts over the first 10 days to establish credibility and engagement:
Post 1: The Launch Announcement (Vision & Honesty) Day 1
Most meat delivery either relies on distant central warehouses with long transit times, or sells cuts that have been thawed and refrozen multiple times. We are building BrisketBox to fix that. By setting up local cold-chain hubs that keep fresh cuts strictly between 0°C and 4°C from dock to doorstep, our goal is to deliver genuine same-day freshness in under 90 minutes. We’re kicking off our rollout in Bengaluru, with Hyderabad and Chennai next on our roadmap. Follow our page to stay updated on our launch journey!
Post 2: Behind the Cold-Chain (Quality & Standards) Day 4
Why temperature matters more than delivery speed: When raw seafood or mutton warms up even slightly during delivery, texture, moisture, and freshness drop rapidly. That's why every BrisketBox delivery uses insulated cold boxes with chilled food-grade gel bricks—keeping your Surmai steaks and tender mutton cuts at pure refrigerator temperature (0°C to 4°C) until you open them at home. Zero shortcuts on freshness.
Post 3: The Sourcing & Butchery Craft Day 8
Daily harbor landings and farm-direct sourcing. Every morning, our catch is selected directly from regional boat landings, hand-trimmed into custom center-cut steaks and curry cuts by skilled butchers, and vacuum-sealed for delivery. Fresh cuts, zero preservatives, and free packaging. Explore our menu live at https://brisketbox.vercel.app
